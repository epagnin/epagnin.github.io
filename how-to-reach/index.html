My office is located in the EDIT Building, room 6482, on the Chalmers Johanneberg Campus.

On Campus

Enter the building via the entrance closest to Quarl Anka’s Väg 2. Once inside, turn left. Shortly after, you will see a large spiral staircase. Take it to floor 6V (not 6Ö). After passing through the glass-and-metal door, my office is the second door on the right. If you take the elevator, use the panel farthest from the entrance and select 6. Exit through the doors opposite where you entered. Otherwise, you will arrive on the Ö side of the building and will need to go half a flight up or down.

My PhD students sit in the office opposite mine; feel free to ask them if my door is closed. If you get lost (which is common), email me with your best guess of where you are, and someone will come find you.

From Göteborg City

Take tram 6, 8 (from Korsvägen), or tram 7, 10 (from near the Central Station) toward Chalmers and get off at Chalmersplatsen. From there, walk uphill into the campus area, and aim at Quarl Anka’s Väg 2. The walk takes about 5–7 minutes.

For a shorter, flatter walk, you can take bus 61 to Engdahlsgatan, or buses 60, 63 to Bergsprängaregatan.

Paying for Public Transportation: You can pay using the Västtrafik To Go app on your smartphone. Alternatively, tap a contactless card (Visa/Mastercard) on the card reader when boarding.
The system automatically charges the correct fare at the end of the day. Multiple taps within the 90-minute ticket window are included at no extra cost. Cash is not accepted.

From the Airport

The easiest option is the airport bus (Vy Flygbussarna). Tickets can be bought online at https://www.flygbussarna.se or at the ticket counter inside the airport, near the exit. Buses run about every 20 minutes, and the trip to Korsvägen takes approximately 25 minutes, from there you can take tram 6 or 8 to reach the campus.
