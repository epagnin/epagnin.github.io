Join the Crypto Team

Great to hear that you are interested in working with me! Lately I am doing research on transparency technologies and digital signatures with advanced properties. Please check my research and recent publications before contacting me.

Note for reaching out in general: I receive many emails every day and cannot reply to most of them. If you do not hear back, please treat this as a polite indication that I do not have the time, funding, or capacity to support your request during the proposed period. For a first contact, always include your updated CV and explain clearly how your interests align with my research and publications. It is also helpful to mention any shared academic connection, as this may provide useful context about your background. I read all email, but a specific, well-prepared message is far more likely to receive proper attention and response.

PhD and Postdocs

Open positions are always advertised through official Chalmers vacancies portal (sieve for ‘Computer Science and Engineering’ under ‘Organization unit level 1’). When a position is open, you can contact me by email, but only applications submitted through the official portal will be considered. If no position is listed on the portal it means there is currently no opening.

Internships and Research Visits

We have previously hosted successful visits through Erasmus, Erasmus+, and traineeship programmes. When contacting me, include your funding situation, proposed dates, research goals, and the topic you would like to work on. It is also helpful to mention any shared academic connection, as this may provide useful context about your background.
