Hi! 👋

I am an Associate Professor (Universitetslektor) at the Computer Science and Engineering department at Chalmers University (Göteborg, Sweden), in the Security & Privacy Lab,
where I lead the Cryptoteam. For more details check out my research page, and the full list of publications with pointers to open access full-texts versions of my works and video recordings when available.

I like research that addresses open challenges and makes feasible what was not feasible before.

If you are interested in joining the Chalmers Cryptoteam, or if you are seeking any form of advising or collaboration, please read this page first before reaching out to me.

I do not intend to accept any new interns, PhD students, or postdocs for 2026.
